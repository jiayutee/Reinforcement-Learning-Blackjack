# 9 October: legal actions and action masking

## Prerequisites

A policy chooses an action from an observation. A Q value estimates expected return for a state-action pair. An action mask tells the policy which actions the environment currently permits. It does not estimate which action is good.

Yesterday's session implemented hit, stand and double. Today [PublicSession.legal_actions](../../public_game/session.py) supplies their shared eligibility rule to both visible snapshots and command validation. Split is still unimplemented and is never advertised.

## Intuition and equations

A policy must not choose double after hitting, or when it cannot afford the extra stake. Let A(s) be the supported legal actions in state s. Greedy selection should use:

`a* = argmax over a in A(s) of Q(s,a)`

Example: Q(hit)=-0.1, Q(stand)=-0.3, Q(double)=+0.2. Before hitting, with enough funds, the greedy choice is double. After a hit, double is excluded; the choice becomes hit. These are invented teaching values, not trained results.

For epsilon-greedy exploration, choose a random action from A(s) with probability epsilon; otherwise choose a greedy legal action. With two legal actions, epsilon=0.1 and a unique greedy action, its probability is 0.9+0.1/2=0.95; the other gets 0.05. Exploration must respect legality too.

A future Q-learning target must maximize over legal next actions. At a terminal state the target is reward alone: do not take a maximum over an empty list. No RL adapter or Q-learning implementation was changed at this checkpoint.

## Code, step by step

1. Ready or settled sessions return an empty player-action list. Deal is a separate wager command, not a member of this list.
2. An active hand supports hit and stand.
3. Add double only for two cards and remaining balance at least the current stake.
4. Return a fresh list in every call and snapshot, so caller edits cannot alter authoritative rules.
5. `act` checks membership in this same list before any candidate draw or debit. A client cannot gain an action by editing its snapshot.

The mask uses phase, visible player cards and balance. Two nonnatural dealer hole cards produce identical active observations and action lists when everything else is equal. Dealer naturals end the round before player decisions under the declared rules; that terminal reveal is intentional.
