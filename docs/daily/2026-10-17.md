
## 03:02 checkpoint: collect 1,000 rounds with a fixed baseline

The [experiment runner](../../public_game/experiment.py) applies one deliberately simple policy: hit below17, otherwise stand. It ignores soft-ace and pair information. It is a baseline for inspecting episodes, not a learning agent or recommended strategy.

Run from the repository root:

```sh
python3 -m public_game.experiment --episodes 1000 --seed 7
```

It reports mean normalized round profit, population standard deviation, wins/losses/pushes, opening naturals and decision transitions. The session uses a persistent seeded shoe and the conservative between-round reserve. A large virtual balance prevents bankroll exhaustion from truncating the requested sample; the balance is not an unlimited-money gameplay recommendation.

Measured results:

| Seed | Rounds | Mean normalized profit | Population SD | Wins | Losses | Pushes | Opening naturals | Decisions |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 7 | 1,000 | -0.0410 | 0.9702 | 409 | 474 | 117 | 101 | 1,441 |
| 19 | 1,000 | -0.0595 | 0.9738 | 405 | 488 | 107 | 89 | 1,443 |

For each run, wins+losses+pushes=round count. The unit of analysis is a round, not an individual split hand or decision. Opening naturals contribute a round outcome but no player-action transition. Standard deviation describes outcome spread, not uncertainty in the estimated mean; it should not be reported as a confidence interval.

The 1.85 percentage point difference between these two samples is compatible with ordinary finite-sample variation. Two seeds do not establish a strategy ranking, edge or profitability. Persistent-shoe rounds are also dependent through shared shoe composition; a later evaluation should use independent shoes or account for within-shoe clustering and report multi-seed uncertainty.

Coding steps: create a session with its own seed and enough virtual balance; repeatedly collect one full round using the threshold callback; sum terminal round outcomes, not hand outcomes; count transitions separately. The policy sees a detached visible snapshot and only selects advertised actions. No value estimates are updated.

All **140 Python tests passed**, including deterministic same-seed output, count conservation, threshold choice and invalid trial counts. Reproduce both runs with the commands above. The collector and runner are documented separately: [episode records](../../public_game/episodes.py).
