# Fixed-policy experiment answers

1,441 decisions are the actions taken over1,000 rounds; rounds can require different numbers of actions, and opening naturals have zero decisions. Each round contributes exactly one final aggregate profit outcome.

Different seeds change cards and episode outcomes. Repeating training and evaluation across independent seeds distinguishes a stable improvement from a lucky sample. Persistent shoes introduce dependence between rounds within a shoe, so independence must be defined at the shoe or shuffle block level, not assumed per hand.
