# Answer — wager affordability

The 5- and 10-chip presets are affordable because their costs are 10 and 20 half-chip units, both at most 37. The 25- and 50-chip presets cost 50 and 100 half-chip units, so they exceed the bankroll. The browser is untrusted and can be bypassed; the Python session must validate typed wagers and presets alike before mutating the game.
