Game rules
  • Six-deck shoe; cards are reshuffled between rounds when needed.
  • Dealer stands on every 17, including soft 17.
  • Natural blackjack pays 3:2; equal naturals push. A 21 after splitting pays 1:1.
  • Double on a two-card hand, including after a split, except split aces.
  • Split equal-rank pairs up to four hands. Split aces get one card each and cannot be resplit.
  • Insurance and surrender are not offered.
